# Literature verification scope

The 59 inherited records are retained with their source-linked metadata audit. This continuation
checks their structural consistency and citation closure against the current manuscript; it does
not claim a fresh full-text reread or live DOI retrieval of every inherited publication.

Seven entries were added after targeted source checking: correctness witnesses (2016),
CPA certification (2014), Verasco (2015), function-contract witnesses (2025 arXiv preprint), and
the official Java SE 21 language specification, Seneca (2024), and DLCDroid (2025). The comparison also revisits the author-hosted
DroidRA journal paper and SOLAR treatment of assumptions. Per-entry metadata and the use of each
reference are recorded in bibliography-audit.csv and literature.csv.

All 66 BibTeX keys are actually cited and compile in both PDF views. There are 60 distinct DOI values and six no-DOI records. The preprint is explicitly labeled as such; the language
specification is a normative document. Metadata/citation checks do not constitute external peer
review or prove the truth of every claim in a cited paper. No inaccessible web request is counted
as successful retrieval.
